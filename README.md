# Sleep Duration and Cardiovascular Health

## Overview

Sleep is an important component of cardiovascular health, yet sleep duration varies considerably among adults. This project uses publicly available data from the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) to investigate the relationship between sleep duration and cardiovascular health in U.S. adults.

## Research Question

Is sleep duration associated with systolic blood pressure and hypertension among U.S. adults?

## Objectives

- Examine the distribution of sleep duration in the study population.
- Investigate the relationship between sleep duration and systolic blood pressure.
- Compare cardiovascular outcomes across sleep-duration groups.
- Evaluate whether associations persist after accounting for demographic and clinical factors.

## Variables of Interest

**Exposure**
- Sleep duration

**Outcomes**
- Systolic blood pressure
- Hypertension status

**Potential Covariates**
- Age
- Sex
- Body mass index (BMI)
- Smoking status

## Planned Analysis

The analysis will be conducted in R and will include data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization, and statistical modeling.

Planned statistical methods include:

- Descriptive statistics
- Data visualization
- Multiple linear regression
- Logistic regression
- Confidence intervals and effect-size interpretation

## Data Source

National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES), Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.
